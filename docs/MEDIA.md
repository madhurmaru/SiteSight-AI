# Fixture media attribution

The bundled construction scene is original programmatically authored SVG artwork in `lib/fixture.ts`, rasterised to JPEG by `lib/demo-seed.ts`. It depicts a fictional construction site and fictional worker figures. No real people, proprietary site photographs, third-party stock media or external image services are used.

The original illustration and deterministic fixture annotations are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The image itself carries a synthetic-evidence watermark. These scenes are illustrative evidence for demonstrating review workflows, not records of real events.

## Evaluation and real-workflow image

The five local evaluation images, including the image used for the verified end-to-end real inspection, come from the test split of the Construction Site Safety dataset mirrored at `keremberke/construction-safety-object-detection`. That dataset is published under CC BY 4.0 and attributed to Roboflow Universe Projects. The downloaded files live under `evaluation/source/images/`; they are evaluation inputs rather than bundled fixture records.
